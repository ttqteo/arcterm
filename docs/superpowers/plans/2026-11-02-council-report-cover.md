# Council report under a cover Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Verify:** `node scripts/verify.mjs ./pkg/council`
**Check:** `NODE_OPTIONS=--max-old-space-size=4096 task check:ts`
**Final:** `node scripts/cdp/final-verify.mjs council-summary council-discussion`
**Prototype:** .superpowers/design/council-report-m/project/M-report-cover.dc.html

**Goal:** Redraw the council report as a report with a cover, swap the "Show the discussion" button for Report / Discussion tabs, and fold the Discussion's header and its seat row into one 52px row.

**Architecture:** One shared tab strip (`counciltabs.tsx`, its count in the pure `counciltabsmodel.ts`) sits in both views. The Summary (`councilsummary.tsx`) loses its header and its side column: one scroll box holds a cover (title, You asked, the answer, a "glance" box with the state, three figures, the panel and the saved document), a sticky tab bar with jump links, and the body (idea cards in a grid, one open at a time with its detail under the grid). The Discussion (`councildiscussion.tsx`) gets a one-row header; the seat chips move into it (`councilseats.tsx`) and leave the chat (`councilchat.tsx`).

**Tech Stack:** React 19, jotai, Tailwind 4 tokens (`frontend/tailwindsetup.css`), lucide-react, vitest, the CDP scenario harness (`scripts/cdp/council/*.mjs`).

**Spec:** the two boards in the Prototype folder: `M-report-cover.dc.html` (the report) and `M1-discussion-tab.dc.html` (the Discussion). Their text comes from a real council; the layout, sizes and tokens are the spec, not the words. Where this plan and a board differ, this plan wins (the board's `⋯` in M1 was replaced by the real Close; the report's `⋯` is the existing ActionsMenu).

## Global Constraints

- Colours only through `@theme` token utilities (DESIGN.md "Do's and Don'ts"); no raw hex/rgba in `className`/`style`. The board's hex values map to tokens: `#262624` surface, `#30302e` surface-raised, `#363532` surface-hover, `#3d3c38` surface-selected, `#383734` edge-mid, `#4a4945` edge-strong, `#2e2d2b` border, `#282725` edge-faint, `#ece9e0` primary, `#c2c0b6` secondary, `#e3e3e3` ink-hi, `#a3a198` muted, `#7b7972` ink-faint, `#d97757` accent.
- Selection and the open card are grey (`surface-selected`, `surface-hover`, `edge-strong`), never the accent; the accent stays on Start an agent on it, Send, links.
- Inter for every label, number and name; `tabular-nums` on counts and figures.
- Every existing `data-council-*` attribute that the scenarios read keeps its name unless a task below renames it; a removed one is removed from the scenarios in the same task.
- Copy: sentence case; the tabs read `Report` and `Discussion`; the Discussion tab's count reads `24 ideas` / `1 idea` and is hidden at 0.
- No text copied from the boards' council into code, tests, fixtures or commit messages (the repo is public): use the scenario fixtures' councils.

## Review Focus

- A council with no summary yet: the Report tab still opens and shows the existing NoSummary state inside the cover's answer slot; the glance box and the tabs still show. (Task 2 step 6: the existing step "a council synthesizing again … or says it is being written" keeps passing, and also checks the glance and the tabs are there.)
- A Review council (no kept ideas, directions past the third have no first step): the cards draw without the First step box and the jump links leave out `kept`. (Task 2 tests for `jumpLinks`, scenario's Review step.)
- A narrow pane (the cockpit's centre at ~760px): the cover's glance box wraps under the text, the card grid drops to one or two columns, the Discussion header's title truncates before the seats or the cost wrap off. (Task 2 step 5 and Task 3 step 2 classes; final screenshots.)
- A seat filter on when the Discussion opens from the report's panel: the header chip shows pressed and the chat shows the "Only …" line with Show everyone. (Task 3 scenario step.)
- Two or more failed seats: Retry all and the per-seat model picks still exist after the seat row is gone. (Task 3 step 3, scenario step on retry all.)
